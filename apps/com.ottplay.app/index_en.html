Widget for launching IPTV player ott-play by Alex on LG webOS.

Telegram channel of the player: t.me/ottplay
Chat: t.me/ottplay_chat

0.2.2 - webOS ipk package version (not a player version)

Widget features:

- the ability to assign an application to the input - the application is launched when the TV is turned on and replaces the standard TV broadcast application, and the player is automatically turned on when exiting other applications (only for webOS 3.5+)
To assign an input, quickly press the yellow and blue buttons on the remote several times. To delete an input, press the green and blue buttons.

- waiting for network readiness - the application checks if the server is available before loading the player
